fittracker

A strength-training log for Android

fittracker is for lifting. Plan your week, follow a program, log every set at the gym with a rest timer between them, and see how your lifts move over the weeks. It also keeps a simple food log and your body weight and measurements.

Everything you record stays on your phone. There is no fittracker account, no advertising and no tracking of how you use the app.

Backup to your own Google Drive

If you choose to, fittracker can back up your data to your own Google Drive. You sign in with your Google account in Settings → Cloud Backup, and fittracker asks Google for two things only: your email address, to show which account is connected, and access to the files fittracker itself creates in your Drive. It cannot see any other file there.

The backups go straight from your phone to your Drive. Restore from Backup brings them back, on the same phone or a new one.

Getting fittracker

fittracker is in a private test on Google Play. People invited by the developer install it from there.
