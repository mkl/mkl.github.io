fittracker — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

fittracker is a strength-training log for Android, published by Henning Merkel ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Your training data stays on your phone

Your workouts, programs and weekly plan, your food log, your body weight and body measurements, and your settings are stored on your phone. I never receive them. There is no fittracker account, no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics of how you use the app. Nothing is sold.

If you export your data, the file goes where you choose, and I never see it. If you back up to Google Drive (below), the backup goes to your own Drive, and I never see it either.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup to Google Drive is off until you turn it on. If you tap Connect Google Drive in Settings → Cloud Backup, Google asks you to sign in and to allow fittracker two things, and only these:

  • Your email address, which the app shows so you can see which Google account is connected. It is kept on your phone only.
  • The files fittracker creates in your Google Drive (Google's "drive.file" access). fittracker cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Once connected, fittracker saves a copy of the data stored in the app as a file in a folder named "FitTracker Backups" in your own Drive: when you tap Back Up Now, and automatically about once a day while you stay connected. It keeps the 7 newest backups and deletes older ones it created. Restore from Backup reads these files back into the app.

The backups travel encrypted (HTTPS) directly between your phone and Google. I never receive your backups, your email address, or anything else from your Google account, and no person reads them. The access Google grants the app is used only to save, list, restore and remove your fittracker backups, at your request or on the daily schedule you turned on.

fittracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That information is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than your backups.

To stop, tap Disconnect in Settings → Cloud Backup: backups stop and the app forgets the connection. To also withdraw fittracker's access from your Google account, remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections. Backups already in your Drive are your files: they stay there until you delete them. Google's handling of your Google account and Drive is described in Google's privacy policy.

What else leaves your phone

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends Google a crash report so I can fix the fault. The report contains the error and the code where it happened, the state of the app at that moment, your phone's model and Android version, the app's version, and a random installation ID. It does not contain your training, food or body data. The Firebase SDK also records when the app is opened, together with app and device information, so that crashes can be counted per session. Google processes this data on my behalf. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Barcode scanning (Google ML Kit)

Barcodes are read on your phone, and camera images are neither stored nor sent anywhere. ML Kit sends Google technical usage statistics: device and app information, performance metrics, error codes and a per-installation identifier. They are used for diagnostics and to improve ML Kit, and they do not identify you.

Product lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a product's barcode, the app sends the barcode to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to fetch the product's name and nutrition. Nothing else is sent. See Open Food Facts' privacy policy at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Your own recipe server (optional)

If you connect your own Tandoor Recipes server in Settings, the app reads your recipes from that server, using the address and token you entered. That connection runs between your phone and your server only.

All of these connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes, and only while you are scanning.
  • Notifications: used for the running workout and its rest timer, and for the body-stats reminder if you turn it on.
  • Internet: used for the connections listed above.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it has stored on your phone. Backups in your Google Drive stay until you delete them from your Drive. Crash reports are deleted by Google after 90 days. To have them deleted sooner, email me with the approximate time of the crash.

Children

fittracker is not directed at children under 13.
